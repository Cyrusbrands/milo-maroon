Title: I built an original 3D island screensaver inspired by Johnny Castaway: Milo Maroon

I missed the feeling of watching a tiny character live his life and unexpectedly do something funny. That became Milo Maroon, a Windows screensaver about a castaway with extremely poor rescue timing.

Milo fishes, cooks, gardens, builds inventions and sometimes sleeps through a cruise ship. Animals have their own movement and quiet periods. Birds can dive for fish; dolphins visit offshore. There are 75 authored activities, a local behavior director and a persistent campsite.

The biggest design lesson was to slow down. Constant gags and constant camera cuts felt repetitive. Letting him simply look around made the next surprise much more enjoyable.

This was made with my creative direction/testing, Codex-assisted programming, AI-assisted references and Tripo-generated models. Models then needed rig adaptation, scale, placement and animation. It runs locally without a model API or account. I want to be clear about the AI workflow rather than pretend I hand-made every asset.

It's an evolving preview, with animation/collision limitations and an unsigned Windows build. Original code is MIT and assets/recordings have separate licenses. The project includes an illustrated story, source and Windows downloads.

Repository: https://github.com/Cyrusbrands/milo-maroon

I'd especially appreciate feedback on animation quality, pacing, performance and accessibility. If you'd like to contribute, the contributor guide and roadmap are in the repository.
