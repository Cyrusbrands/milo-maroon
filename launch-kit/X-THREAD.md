# X launch thread — draft, not posted

Attach launch-kit/milo-maroon-dolphins.mp4 to post 1. Use the two prototype/current island images with post 3. Posts below are intentionally short; verify the final link before publishing.

1. I wanted the feeling of Johnny Castaway back. So I started building Milo Maroon: a tiny 3D island where a castaway cooks, invents, befriends animals… and sometimes sleeps through a passing cruise ship. First public preview 🏝️

2. The most useful feedback I gave the project was: let him do nothing sometimes. Less camera movement. Fewer cruises. More time to look around. A surprise works better when the island has room to breathe.

3. It started with simple palms, a tent and a little procedural character. Then came character sheets, Tripo models and a LOT of screenshots asking: why is he swimming on sand? Where did his hands go? Why is that ship going backwards?

4. The models weren't the finish line. They needed scale, rig adaptation, prop grips, water placement and animation. Higher polygon counts don't fix a hand pointing the wrong way. Testing and iteration do.

5. Now birds and fish change routes, leave and return. A seabird might dive for lunch. Dolphins play offshore. There are quiet spells, odd visitors, a growing campsite and 75 authored activities guided by a local behavior simulation.

6. Transparent about the workflow: my creative direction and testing, Codex-assisted code, AI-assisted references, Tripo-generated 3D models, and recordings I supplied. No LLM or subscription is needed to run the finished application.

7. It's an evolving Windows x64 preview, not a finished commercial game. Some animations still need polish. The code is MIT; art and recordings have separate licenses. Bug reports, animation fixes, accessibility and performance work are all welcome.

8. Download, read the illustrated story or help improve the island:
https://github.com/Cyrusbrands/milo-maroon

If Milo makes you smile, a star helps other people find him. 🏝️
