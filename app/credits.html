A world worth listening to.

The soundscape combines locally bundled recordings under the licenses below, procedural effects and original procedural music.

Ocean waves

“Oceanwavescrushing.ogg” — Luftrum, recorded 16 February 2008 at Kalundborg Fjord, Røsnæs. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oceanwavescrushing.ogg
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The original file is unmodified. Playback is looped, offset and volume-mixed in the soundscape. The creator does not endorse this application.

Rain

“Rain.ogg” — ジダネ, recorded 4 April 2007. Released into the public domain by the recording’s author.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain.ogg

The file is unmodified; playback is looped and mixed according to the island’s weather.

Graphics and software

Original procedural island graphics, characters, animation, behaviour simulation and music. Three.js is used under its bundled MIT license. The Windows host uses Microsoft WebView2; its license and notices are included.

No original Sierra artwork, executable code or audio is used.

Recorded island effects

Additional recordings supplied by the project owner from Pixabay, adapted into short effects and loops for the island’s events and activities.

These recordings are licensed separately from the code under the Pixabay Content License. The retained source inventory is included with the Windows release.
